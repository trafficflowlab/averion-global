Schemes & Programmes

Government & Institutional Funding We Support

Businesses often hear about many different government initiatives but are not sure which one fits as per their business situation.

We help you review your options for available government funding and determine which one suitable for your business before applying.

Mudra Yojana (Tarun)

Loans between ₹5 Lakhs and ₹10 Lakhs under the Tarun category of the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana for eligible micro and small businesses.

PMEGP

Margin money subsidy provided by the government for eligible projects up to ₹50 Lakhs in manufacturing and ₹20 Lakhs in service sectors.

CGTMSE Guarantee

The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises enables banks to give collateral free loans to eligible MSMEs.

Stand-Up India

A scheme that facilitates bank loans from ₹10 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore for eligible SC/ST and women entrepreneurs to establish new enterprises.

Interest Subvention Scheme

An interest subvention of 2% per annum on eligible MSME loans, as per the government's current policy guidelines.

State MSME Incentives

Many state governments provide various incentives such as capital subsidies, tax benefits etc. through their respective policies, subject to eligibility and state specific rules.
